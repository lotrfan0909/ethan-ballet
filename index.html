More than a performance

Ten years in the making.

“The goal isn't just to stand on a stage. It's to earn the chance to keep coming back to it.”

I've spent the last ten years building my life around ballet. What began as a passion has grown into a serious commitment: long hours in the studio, constant correction, physical work, and the determination to keep improving even when progress is difficult to see.

I'm currently part of AMB Junior Company, training on a pre-professional track. I want to take that training as far as it can go and earn a place in the professional ballet world.

This path takes more than talent. It takes consistent training, opportunities to learn from experienced teachers, auditions, travel, and the ability to keep showing up. I'm working toward all of it one step at a time.
